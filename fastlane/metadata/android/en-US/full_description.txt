<p>VPN client for your HTTPS, SOCKS5, MASQUE α (HTTP/3) or SSH server. Supports HTTPS/SSH jump hosts with separate credentials per hop, split tunneling and DoH.</p>

<p>Profiles, import/export, configuration subscriptions, automatic failover, connection and speed tests, VPN visibility and IP reputation.</p>

<p>No account, ads or analytics. Credentials are encrypted at rest; logs stay local unless shared. Diagnostics contact external services.</p>

<p>Servers are not included. SOCKS5 does not encrypt its connection or credentials. MASQUE is experimental.</p>
