<p>MegaProxy routes all or selected Android apps through your HTTPS, SOCKS5, MASQUE α (HTTP/3) or SSH server, including HTTPS and SSH jump hosts for multi-hop connections. Servers and credentials are not included.</p>

<ul>
<li>HTTPS and SSH jump hosts with separate authentication and trust checks per hop</li>
<li>Split tunneling, local-network bypass, optional IPv6 and Always-on VPN</li>
<li>HTTP/2 multiplexing, experimental HTTP/3 TCP/UDP</li>
<li>DNS-over-HTTPS and configurable TLS/QUIC/SSH fingerprints</li>
<li>Profiles, import/export, configuration subscriptions and automatic failover</li>
<li>Connection and UDP tests, download/upload speed tests, traffic metrics and VPN visibility with IP reputation</li>
<li>Encrypted stored credentials, local logs, English and Russian interfaces</li>
</ul>

<p>No account, ads, analytics or remote telemetry. Diagnostics contact external services; logs are shared only when you choose.</p>

<p>SOCKS5 does not encrypt its connection or credentials. HTTPS over TCP and SSH do not forward arbitrary UDP. MASQUE is experimental with packet-size and server limits. Fingerprints approximate handshakes, not complete browser behaviour.</p>
