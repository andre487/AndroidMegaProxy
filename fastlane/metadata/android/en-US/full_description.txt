<p>MegaProxy is an open-source Android VPN client for reliable and secure connections through proxy servers you control or trust.</p>

<p>It contains no advertising, analytics SDKs, tracking identifiers, or remote telemetry. Connection statistics, profiles, and diagnostic logs remain on the device unless you explicitly choose to share them.</p>

<p>Features include:</p>

<ul>
<li>HTTPS proxies over TLS using CONNECT without intercepting application traffic</li>
<li>HTTP/2 CONNECT multiplexing with automatic HTTP/1.1 fallback</li>
<li>SSH transport and SSH through a jump host</li>
<li>Password and unencrypted private-key SSH authentication</li>
<li>Global VPN and per-application split tunneling</li>
<li>Android Always-on VPN integration</li>
<li>DNS-over-HTTPS with configurable providers and fallback</li>
<li>Configurable HTTPS and SSH client fingerprints</li>
<li>Multiple profiles, import, export, diagnostics, and automatic failover</li>
<li>English and Russian interfaces</li>
</ul>

<p>MegaProxy does not provide a proxy service. You need an HTTPS, SSH, or SSH-with-jump server that you operate or trust.</p>

<p>Only TCP application traffic is forwarded. General UDP and QUIC forwarding are not supported.</p>
