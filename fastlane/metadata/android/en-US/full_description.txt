<p>Open-source VPN client for your HTTPS, SOCKS5, MASQUE α (HTTP/3) or SSH server. HTTPS/SSH Jump supports two hops with separate authentication and trust checks. Servers and credentials are not included.</p>

<p><b>Optional app ownership checks on Android 10+</b>: Standard and Advanced levels check TCP/UDP sources and block ping inside the VPN. Off by default; applies on reconnection. May delay TCP setup, slow UDP or disrupt apps whose owner cannot be identified.</p>

<ul>
<li>Route all apps or selected apps; local-network bypass and optional IPv6.</li>
<li>Profiles, import/export, HTTPS configuration subscriptions, automatic failover and Android Always-on VPN.</li>
<li>DoH by default, optional plain TCP/UDP DNS, TLS/QUIC/SSH presets and custom JA3.</li>
<li>Connection diagnostics and download/upload speed tests with a shared exportable log; VPN visibility and IP reputation.</li>
<li>Traffic totals/rates in IEC or SI, passive TCP/QUIC metrics, F-Droid/GitHub update checks, English and Russian interfaces.</li>
<li>Optional HTTPS authentication diagnostics using an operator-provided knock host.</li>

<p>No account, ads or analytics. Passwords and private keys are encrypted at rest; logs stay local unless shared. Diagnostics, enabled app-update checks and configured subscriptions contact external services. SOCKS5 does not encrypt its connection or credentials. MASQUE is experimental.</p>
